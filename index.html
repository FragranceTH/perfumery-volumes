Look before you buy

Every material gets the same six questions.

What does it smell like at a concentration you can use? What is it doing in the formula, and at what percentage? Where does it come from, and how is it farmed or made? What mood does it occupy? And what goes wrong when you use it.

That uniformity is the point: it lets you compare a bergamot, a musk and a molecule from a laboratory fairly, in the same shape, on facing pages.

Questions

Before you buy.

What exactly do I get?

Two PDF files, A4, designed for reading on screen and printing. Volume I is 39 pages, Volume II is 107. Both are yours to keep, with free revisions when they are updated.

What is your returns policy?

All sales are final, because the files arrive instantly and cannot be returned. The six-page sample and the complete contents list are free to review first — and if something is still unclear, email and ask before you buy.

I have never made perfume. Is this too advanced?

No. Volume I assumes no chemistry and starts with what the industry actually is and how smell behaves. Chapter 8 is the only maths, and it is worked line by line. The codex is reference — you dip into it as you buy materials.

Does it cover IFRA and the regulations?

Yes — chapters 14 and 15 cover IFRA, allergen declaration and what each market requires, and it deliberately teaches you how to read a current certificate rather than printing limits that go out of date. It is educational, not legal advice.

How is it delivered?

Immediately after payment: a download link on screen and by email. No account needed, no app.

Can I print it or use it in a class?

Print it for your own bench, yes. For teaching, workshops or resale, get in touch — there is a simple licence for that.

Are the material photographs real?

Seventy-four are photographic plates of the material as it arrives. The pure aroma chemicals — vanillin, the aldehydes, the musks — get a drawn specimen plate captioned with their actual form, because a photograph of thirty identical vials would tell you nothing.
